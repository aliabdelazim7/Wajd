# Wajd Agency — Customer Psychology, Emotional UX & Conversion Experience Audit

**Date:** August 21, 2026  
**Auditor:** Manus AI (Customer Experience Researcher & Behavioral UX Specialist)  
**Objective:** Evaluate if the website effectively converts first-time visitors into high-intent leads for the Gulf market.

---

## 1. The 5-Second First Impression Test

### What I understood in 5 seconds:
*   **Who is Wajd?** A tech-enabled growth agency for the Gulf market.
*   **What do they sell?** Integrated systems (Marketing + Tech Infrastructure) to scale revenue.
*   **For whom?** E-commerce stores and ambitious brands in KSA and UAE.
*   **Why care?** They don't just "run ads"; they build the engine (Salla/Zid/Shopify + Systems) that makes ads profitable.

### 5-Second Understanding Statement:
> "Wajd is a premium growth partner that engineers technical and marketing systems for Gulf-based stores to turn ad spend into measurable net profit."

---

## 2. Emotional Assessment

### Primary Emotions: **Premium**, **Confidence**, **Curiosity**.

*   **Why Premium?** The dark-mode aesthetic with refined gold accents and high-end typography immediately signals a "heavyweight" player. It doesn't look like a generic agency template.
*   **Why Confidence?** The bold display of results ($12M+ managed, 6.4x peak ROAS) provides instant social proof that offsets the risk of a new partnership.
*   **Why Curiosity?** The "Impact Simulator" and the phrasing "نُهندس نموّك" (We engineer your growth) make me want to see how they would apply this logic to *my* specific business.

---

## 3. Emotional Journey Mapping

| Section | Emotion Before | Emotion After | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero** | Neutral | Curiosity | The cinematic feel and bold headline "Engineering Growth" spark interest. |
| **Results Bar** | Curiosity | Confidence | Tangible numbers ($12M+, 120K conversions) validate the curiosity. |

---

## 4. Customer Psychology Funnel

1.  **Attention (High):** The visual hierarchy is excellent. Attention goes from the Headline -> Results -> Simulator.
2.  **Understanding (High):** Clear distinction between "Acquisition" and "Infrastructure."
3.  **Relevance (High):** Specific mentions of Riyadh/Dubai and Salla/Zid make it feel tailor-made for a GCC business owner.
4.  **Trust (High):** Built through case studies, partner logos, and transparency (24/7 data dashboard promise).
5.  **Desire (Strong):** The "Growth Engine" model makes the service feel like a custom-built asset rather than a generic monthly fee.
6.  **Action (Clear):** "Map your growth roadmap" is a lower-friction CTA than "Contact Us," encouraging more clicks.

---

## 5. "Would I Trust Them?"

**Answer: Yes.**

*   **Increase Trust:** Specificity of the tech stack (Salla/Zid), real-world software previews (POS/Admin), and localized results.
*   **Reduce Trust:** The Simulator is great but needs to keep its "Estimate" disclaimer visible to avoid sounding like a "get rich quick" tool. (Currently handled well with the asterisk note).

---

## 6. Premium Perception & Brand Distinctiveness

*   **Feel:** Strategic, Expert, Reliable.
*   **Distinctiveness:** High. If I replaced "Wajd" with another name, the design would still feel premium, but the **"Engineering"** concept and the **"Modular Growth Engine"** are uniquely tied to the Wajd brand identity. It feels like an *architectural firm* for growth, not a marketing shop.

---

## 7. Persona Testing

### Persona B — Ecommerce Owner (Target Hit)
*   **Catch:** Salla/Zid integration and ROAS-focused content.
*   **Action:** They will likely use the Builder to see what a "Growth Plan + Store Launch" costs.

### Persona E — Skeptical Owner (Target Hit)
*   **Catch:** The "Outcome Engine" section which calls out "Vanity Metrics" (Clicks/Likes).
*   **Action:** They will appreciate the "24/7 Data Transparency" promise.

---

## 8. Time-Based Comprehension Tests

*   **10-Second Test:** "Wajd is a **tech-growth** company that helps **Gulf stores** achieve **measurable revenue**."
*   **30-Second Test:** I know they do POS systems, AI automation, and ROI-marketing for Salla/Zid. They are different because they build the systems themselves. I should click the builder to start.
*   **60-Second Test:** I am ready to request a setup. The trust is high enough to share my business details.

---

## 9. Emotional Peaks & Friction

*   **WOW Moment:** The **Impact Simulator**. It’s rare for an agency to give an interactive estimate upfront. It feels modern and "tech-first."
*   **Friction Moment:** The **Growth Engine Builder** is very powerful but has many options. The "Request this setup" button is the right move to transition from "browsing" to "consulting" before things get too complex.

---

## Final Recommendation:
The site is **highly effective**. It successfully transitions from a "Premium Visual" to a "Strategic Value" proposition. It positions Wajd as a "heavyweight" partner that understands both the tech and the economics of the Gulf market.

**Next Steps for even higher conversion:**
1.  **Video Proof:** Add short "Screen Recordings" of the Adria/Market POS systems in action to make the "Tech Solutions" feel even more tangible.
2.  **Intent Feed:** Use the newly implemented Analytics Dashboard to see which "Technical Modules" are toggled most in the builder and create specific landing pages for them.
