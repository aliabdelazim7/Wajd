# Growth Insights research notes — 21 Aug 2026

## Primary source: Saudi Internet Report 2025

The Communications, Space and Technology Commission (CST) reports the following headline figures for Saudi Arabia in 2025:

- Internet penetration: **99.6%**.
- Internet users spending 7 hours or more online daily: **61.3%**.
- AI-tool adoption rate: **45.2%**, described by CST as more than double the previous year.
- Average monthly mobile internet consumption: **53 GB per person**.
- Median mobile download speed: **216 Mbps**, ranking among the top five in the G20.
- Online shoppers: **76.9%**.
- Mobile-phone internet use: **99.6%**; computer use: **47.4%**; tablet use: **39.2%**.
- Local websites are used by **95.3%** of online shoppers, while international websites are used by **55.4%**.
- Most-used social platforms listed by CST include WhatsApp (92.7%), Snapchat (83.6%), TikTok (77%), YouTube (75.8%), and Instagram (40.8%).
- Peak internet usage is reported as 9–11 pm, with Friday as the peak day.

Source URL: https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/knowledge-center/reports/saudi-internet-report-25-dashboard
