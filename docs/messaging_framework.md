# Wajd Agency Messaging Framework

## Brand Essence
**Wajd** is a "Growth Engineering" partner. We don't just run ads; we build the digital infrastructure (stores, systems, automation) that makes those ads profitable.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Arabic**: نُهندس البنية التقنية، لنُوجد الأثر التسويقي. (We engineer the tech to create the marketing impact).
- **English**: We build the tech that powers your growth.

## Target Personas

### 1. The Ambitious Launcher (Startups/New Brands)
- **Pain**: "I have a great product but my website looks amateur and I don't know how to start ads."
- **Message**: "Professional launch, Gulf-ready tech, and a marketing plan that grows with you."
- **Outcome**: Credibility + First Sales.

### 2. The Scaling Store (Established E-commerce)
- **Pain**: "I'm spending a lot on ads but my ROAS is low and my site is slow/unreliable."
- **Message**: "Fix your tech leaks and optimize your performance with data-driven engineering."
- **Outcome**: Higher ROAS + Reliable Operations.

### 3. The Efficient Operator (Service/Maintenance Businesses)
- **Pain**: "We lose leads in WhatsApp/Telegram and our reporting is manual and slow."
- **Message**: "AI-powered automation that turns chat reports into organized tickets instantly."
- **Outcome**: 0% Lead Loss + Instant Response.

## Tone of Voice
- **Professional**: We know the Gulf market (Saudi/UAE).
- **Direct**: No marketing fluff. We talk about ROI and Profits.
- **Supportive**: We are a partner, not just a vendor.
- **Clever**: Using the "Wajd" brand name subtly (Found/Create).

## Key Terms Refinement
- **Instead of "Growth Audit" (تدقيق النمو)**: Use "Growth Roadmap" (خارطة طريق النمو) or "Opportunity Analysis" (تحليل الفرص).
- **Instead of "Acquisition Engineering"**: Use "Customer Magnet Systems" (أنظمة جذب العملاء).
- **Instead of "Revenue Engineering"**: Use "Profit-First Strategy" (استراتيجية الربح أولاً).

## Modular Growth Engine Offer

Wajd should present its commercial offer as a configurable system rather than a wall of fixed packages. The visitor first chooses a monthly **core growth plan**—Starter at SAR 350, Growth at SAR 950, or Partner at SAR 2,200—then adds only the technical modules required at the current stage of the business.

The initial add-on catalog includes professional store launch for Salla, Zid, or Shopify from SAR 1,800 one-time; Market POS from SAR 2,500 one-time; LiftDesk AI automation from SAR 750 monthly; custom systems from SAR 4,500 one-time; and a performance dashboard from SAR 600 one-time. These are **indicative starting points**, not binding quotes. The final scope and price are confirmed after reviewing the client's workflow, integrations, and delivery requirements.

The conversion path is intentionally simple: **choose the core, add the modules, request the setup**. The builder sends the selected configuration to the contact form, stores it with the lead, and includes it in the email and Telegram notification so the sales conversation starts with context instead of a blank inquiry.
